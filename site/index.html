How to read a result here

Predict first

Each item commits a prediction, attack set, sample size and pass bar before its first run. Misses are reported.

Three measures, together

Attack success, retained utility, and cost. A defense that refuses everything scores zero utility and says so.

Live vs scripted

Live means a real model produced a saved run. Scripted illustrates a mechanism and never counts.

Failures stay in

Ideas that did not work get the same write-up as the ones that did. Open an item above to see what would close its gaps.
